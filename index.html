Meiguang Jin

I lead the Live AIGC team at Alibaba Taobao, building digital human systems for live commerce — covering the full technology stack from multimodal live-stream understanding to generation, spanning LLM agents, speech synthesis, video generation, and the product & engineering infrastructure that brings them to production.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Bern (Switzerland) with Prof. Paolo Favaro, focusing on computational photography and low-level vision. I've published 15+ papers at CVPR, ECCV, NeurIPS, AAAI, etc., with 2 Spotlight selections. I joined Alibaba in 2019 through the Alibaba Star program.
